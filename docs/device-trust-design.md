# Device trust design exercise

The future design would obtain managed-device state from a trusted endpoint-management platform. The IdP would evaluate an appropriate device-assurance policy and provide a documented recovery/exception path. Authentication may need stronger controls for sensitive resources.

Browser headers or a user-editable attribute are not a trustworthy substitute for managed posture. Jamf, Island, Google Workspace and Entra device compliance are not implemented by this lab. A future proof of concept needs authentic endpoint signals, owner-approved policy and positive/negative unmanaged-device tests.

**Status: design only.**
