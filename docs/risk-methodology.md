# Risk assessment method

This is a qualitative lab method, not a claim to implement a complete enterprise risk management framework.

## Scales

Likelihood: Low means limited exposure and prerequisites; Medium means plausible during normal lab use; High means likely without additional controls. Impact: Low means limited reversible inconvenience; Medium means access disruption or contained unauthorized access; High means privileged compromise, broad exposure or inability to remove sensitive access.

| Likelihood / impact | Low | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | Low | Low | Medium |
| Medium | Low | Medium | High |
| High | Medium | High | High |

Assess inherent risk before treatment and residual risk after checking the control. Ratings in the starter register are proposed design assessments. Residual ratings remain unassessed until evidence exists.

Every item records scenario, owner, treatment, validation and status. Treatments can reduce, avoid, transfer or accept risk. This solo lab has no real corporate risk-acceptance authority; role-played decisions must be labelled accordingly. Reassess after architecture changes, failures or missing control evidence.
